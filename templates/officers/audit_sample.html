Random call-back sample

The department draws this sample, not the provider — that is the whole point. Each sampled record stores the probability p of being drawn, so its weight is 1 ÷ p. Weighted results then represent every trainee, not only the ones who answered.

For the 36-hour MVP the sample is drawn and the weights are applied to the reported rates. The independent telephone calls themselves are a Pilot-phase activity.
