A consent-based longitudinal outcome tracking system that links every certification to a verified employment record, grades every claim E0–E4, and shows reach honestly beside every rate.

This is a layer on top of Skill India Digital and Mahaswayam. It does not replace them; it answers the question neither of them answers — what happened to the trainee after the certificate was issued?

Why the numbers you see carry a reach rate

Follow-up replies run at roughly 20–30% (NCVER benchmark). A 42% placement rate on its own says nothing about the 58% you never heard from. So every rate in this system is printed next to its response rate and its grade mix, and a department-drawn random sample of non-repliers is weighted back in.

Never a bare percentage — the partial that renders every rate lives at templates/dashboard/_rate.html.

Consent first, and withdrawable

Four purposes, each toggled separately at enrolment: follow-up contact, employer validation, record match, and statistics. Withdrawing one purpose stops only that purpose. "My Data" lets a trainee correct their details, withdraw consent, or request erasure through an OTP check.

No Aadhaar number is stored anywhere in this system.

Skill gaps need two agreeing signals

A course is flagged only where at least two of three signals agree: vacancies exist for that occupation in the district, employers report "skills lacking" for its graduates, or trainees themselves say so. One signal is never enough to redesign a course.

Evidence grades, not self-reported numbers

E0 trainee self-report · E1 officer confirmed · E2 employer confirmed · E3 document seen · E4 matched to an EPFO/ESIC record. Any dashboard can be filtered to a minimum grade, so you can see what the rate looks like counting only employer-confirmed outcomes.
